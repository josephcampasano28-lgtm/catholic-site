✝ Growing in the Faith

Simple ways to grow closer to God, based on the Catechism of the Catholic Church.

Prayer

Prayer is a relationship with God, not just a list of requests. Set aside a little time each day, even five minutes.

CCC 2558-2565

The Church encourages us to pray throughout the day, offering ordinary moments like work, meals, and travel to God.

CCC 2742-2745

The Sacraments

The Eucharist is the center of Catholic life. Going to Mass on Sundays is how we gather as a community and receive Christ.

CCC 1324, 2180-2183

Confession (Reconciliation) is how we receive God's mercy and forgiveness for our sins and start fresh.

CCC 1422

Scripture

Reading the Bible regularly helps us know Christ. The Church encourages all the faithful to read it often.

CCC 131-133

Living Your Faith

Charity, loving God above all and our neighbor as ourselves, is the heart of the Christian life.

CCC 1822

The works of mercy, like feeding the hungry, visiting the sick, and forgiving others, are practical ways to live out your faith.

CCC 2447
